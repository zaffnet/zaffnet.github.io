About Me

I am an NLP researcher and engineer who enjoys building systems that feel thoughtful to the people who rely on them. Whether I am refining a retrieval-augmented workflow or scaling a speech recognizer, I focus on pairing rigorous experimentation with intuitive product experiences.

Beyond work, I am curious about philosophy of science, linguistics, and behavioural economics—disciplines that influence how I frame problems and evaluate the impact of AI-driven solutions.

Timeline

2023 – 2025

Master of Applied Science in Computer Engineering

University of Toronto

My graduate research explored how generative agents can deliver consistent motivational interviewing support to smokers preparing to quit. The resulting thesis presents a fully generative counsellor that balances empathy and clinical fidelity.

I deployed the multi-agent counsellor on AWS ECS with LangChain LCEL orchestration, used ReAct strategies to pick the right counselling behaviours in real time, and built a validation workflow that installs human smoker personas into synthetic ``doppelgängers'' for safe experimentation.

Dialpad Canada Inc.

Highlights include shipping a bilingual sentiment classifier that matched Spanish performance without sacrificing English accuracy, and developing an n-gram boosting approach that improved domain keyword recognition by 26%.

I also unified punctuation and casing into a single BERT model, cutting inference latency by 4% and reducing errors, scaled grapheme-to-phoneme modelling with Hugging Face Transformers for a 70% accuracy lift, and engineered supporting platforms—from an Angular-based annotation studio to Kubeflow pipelines that automated data hygiene and de-anonymization.
