About Me

I am a Natural Language Processing (NLP) engineer and researcher with a passion for developing cutting-edge AI applications that solve real-world problems. My experience spans the full development lifecycle, from conceptualizing and designing models to deploying them in scalable, real-time environments.

I recently graduated with a Master of Applied Science in Computer Engineering from the University of Toronto, where my research focused on creating a fully generative therapeutic chatbot to help individuals with smoking cessation. This project involved building a multi-agent system, implementing advanced frameworks like ReAct, and leveraging technologies such as LangChain and ChromaDB to improve the chatbot's effectiveness.

Before my Master's, I spent over four years at Dialpad, where I worked on a variety of NLP challenges. I have experience in fine-tuning sentiment analysis models, optimizing inference latency for real-time applications, and building data annotation platforms. I am proficient in a wide range of tools and technologies, including PyTorch, HuggingFace, and the AWS and GCP cloud platforms.

I am always eager to learn and apply new techniques to create impactful AI solutions. I am particularly interested in the fields of generative AI, MLOps, and the application of NLP in healthcare.
